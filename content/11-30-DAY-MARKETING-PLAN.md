# SHADEO: 30-day launch marketing plan

**Goal:** first 100 orders in 30 days.
**Suggested budget:** Tk 40,000–60,000 for ads + Tk 15,000–25,000 for influencers + product samples. Scale whatever works.

**Launch offer:** `LAUNCH WEEK: 15% OFF EVERYTHING + FREE DELIVERY` (days 8–14), then normal pricing with "Buy 2, get 15% off".

---

## Week 1 (days 1–7): Warm-up, before launch
**Goal:** build 1,000+ followers and a waitlist before the store opens.

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 1 | Set up Facebook Page, Instagram and TikTok with the same handle. Bio: "Premium eyewear · Designed in Dhaka · Launching [date] 👓" |
| 1–2 | Shoot content: 30+ photos and 15 short videos of the frames (on models, unboxing, close-ups, UV test) |
| 2 | Post 1: brand teaser ("Something bright is coming…") with a blurred product photo |
| 3 | Post 2: founder story reel: "Why I started SHADEO" (30 sec, face to camera; this builds trust) |
| 3 | Create a **Facebook/Instagram "waitlist" lead ad** (Tk 500/day): "Get early access + 20% off launch day" |
| 4 | Post 3: UV400 test reel: shine a UV torch through a SHADEO lens vs a cheap street pair |
| 5 | Influencer outreach: DM 30 micro-influencers (5k–50k followers) in fashion, lifestyle, travel, cars and tech. Offer a free pair + a 10% code for their followers |
| 6 | Post 4: "Which shape are you?" carousel (face-shape guide) to drive saves and shares |
| 7 | Post 5: countdown story + "Tomorrow" post. Email/SMS the waitlist: "Doors open tomorrow at 8 PM" |

## Week 2 (days 8–14): Launch week
**Goal:** first 40 orders.

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 8 | **LAUNCH at 8 PM.** Post the launch reel, go live on Facebook/Instagram for 20 minutes showing frames and answering questions. Send the waitlist their 20% early-access code |
| 8 | Start **sales campaign** (Meta Advantage+ Shopping or Sales objective): Tk 1,500–2,000/day, 3–5 ad creatives (see below) |
| 9 | Start a TikTok Spark Ads campaign boosting your best-performing organic video: Tk 800/day |
| 9–14 | Influencer posts go live (aim for 2 per day). Repost all of them in stories |
| 10 | Facebook groups: post (where allowed) in fashion, travel and car groups, and answer "where to buy sunglasses" questions helpfully |
| 11 | Post: first customer unboxing / review (ask your first buyers for a photo in return for 100 loyalty points) |
| 12 | Story: "Only X left of the Gulshan Aviator", but only if true |
| 14 | Last day of launch offer: "Ends at midnight" email + SMS + story |

**Ad creatives to test (vertical 9:16 and square 1:1):**
1. **UV test video:** torch + lens, "Is your sunglass actually protecting you?"
2. **Try-on reel:** 1 model, 6 frames, quick cuts to a trending audio, "Which one? 1–6"
3. **Problem/solution:** squinting in Dhaka traffic, then putting on polarized Gulshan
4. **Unboxing:** case, cloth, card, frames, "Tk 2,490. Cash on Delivery."
5. **Static offer image:** product on amber background, "BUY 2, GET 15% OFF · FREE DELIVERY"

## Week 3 (days 15–21): Optimize
**Goal:** find your winning ad and product.

| Task | Details |
|---|---|
| Kill losers | Pause any ad with cost per purchase above Tk 600 after Tk 2,000 spend |
| Scale winners | Raise the budget 20–30% every 2 days on ads under Tk 400 per purchase |
| Retargeting ads | Tk 500/day aimed at website visitors and video viewers from the last 14 days: "Still thinking? Free delivery + 7-day returns" |
| Content | Post daily: 1 reel + 3 stories. Reuse customer photos (with permission) |
| Blog | Publish "Which sunglasses suit my face shape?" and "Polarized vs non-polarized" |
| Collect reviews | WhatsApp every delivered customer: "How do you like them? Send a photo for 100 points 🎁" |
| COD quality | Track the return/refusal rate by area; require advance delivery charge for repeat refusers |

## Week 4 (days 22–30): Retain & expand
**Goal:** repeat purchases + referrals.

| Task | Details |
|---|---|
| Referral launch | "Give 10%, Get 10%" email to all customers |
| Prescription push | Ad + post: "Your prescription, in style, from Tk 3,490 with lenses" |
| Blue-light campaign | Target students and office workers: "8 hours on a screen? Your eyes deserve this." |
| Influencer round 2 | Re-book the 3 best-performing influencers (by code usage); try 1 bigger creator (100k+) |
| Bundle offer | "Couple's pack: 2 pairs + free cases". Promote for weekends/Valentine's/Eid as relevant |
| Review the month | Check: total orders, ad spend per order, best-selling frames, return rate, top traffic source. Reorder the winners, discount the slow movers |

## Daily content ideas (TikTok / Reels)
1. "Rating sunglasses for every face shape" (use staff/friends)
2. "Tk 300 street sunglasses vs SHADEO: UV test"
3. "POV: you finally found glasses that suit your round face"
4. "Packing your order" ASMR (show real orders, with customer names hidden)
5. "Which frame for which outfit?": panjabi, saree, office, gym
6. "Guess the price" street interviews in Dhanmondi/Gulshan
7. Before/after: glare vs polarized view through the phone camera
8. "Sunglasses that survive Dhaka traffic": durability bend test on TR90
9. Customer duets / stitches reacting to reviews
10. "How to find your frame size in 10 seconds" (credit-card trick)

## Influencer brief (send with the free pair)
- 1 reel or TikTok + 3 stories within 7 days of delivery
- Show the unboxing, try-on, and one real-life moment (driving, café, beach)
- Mention: 100% UV400, Cash on Delivery, free delivery over Tk 3,000
- Personal code `[NAME]10` for 10% off (track sales per influencer)
- Tag @shadeo.bd and add the link in bio/story
- Mark the post as a paid partnership or say it's a gifted pair (required for honest advertising)

| Conversion rate | 1.5%+ |
