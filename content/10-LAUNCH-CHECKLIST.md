# SHADEO: Launch checklist

Tick everything before you remove the store password (**Online Store → Preferences → Password protection**).

## Business & legal
- [ ] Trade license and e-TIN ready (and e-commerce registration if required for your business)
- [ ] Business bank account / bKash merchant account
- [ ] All 5 policies published (Shipping, Returns, Warranty, Privacy, Terms) and linked in the footer
- [ ] Business name, address, phone and email shown on the Contact page

## Domain & email
- [ ] Custom domain connected and set as **primary** (Settings → Domains)
- [ ] SSL shows the padlock (automatic, but check)
- [ ] `hello@yourdomain` email works (Google Workspace, Zoho, or forwarding)
- [ ] Sender email set in Settings → Notifications

## Products
- [ ] At least 15–20 products live, each with 4+ photos (front, 45°, side, on a model)
- [ ] Every product has: price, compare-at price (if on sale), SKU, weight, inventory, type, tags, metafields (sizes)
- [ ] Eyeglasses tagged `lens-selector`; `lens-upgrade` product active but hidden from collections
- [ ] All collections show the right products, and no empty collections are linked in the menu
- [ ] Unique description + SEO title/description on every product

## Payments (test each one)
- [ ] Cash on Delivery appears at checkout
- [ ] bKash / Nagad / card gateway works. Place a **real Tk 10 test order** and refund it
- [ ] Order confirmation email arrives, with correct branding and logo
- [ ] Test discount codes `WELCOME10`, `COMEBACK10` and the automatic "Buy 2, get 15%"

## Shipping
- [ ] Inside Dhaka Tk 70, Outside Dhaka Tk 130, Free over Tk 3,000: test all three in checkout
- [ ] Free-delivery bar in the cart drawer matches (Tk 3,000)
- [ ] Courier account active, pickup address set, packaging stocked
- [ ] Delivery estimate snippet numbers match your courier

## Theme & features
- [ ] Homepage: every section filled in, no Dawn placeholder images left
- [ ] Mega menu links all work (click every single one)
- [ ] Lens selector: choose each lens → cart shows the frame + Lens Upgrade line, with properties
- [ ] Prescription "Type it in now" fields are required and saved on the order
- [ ] Size guide pop-up opens and closes
- [ ] Sticky add-to-cart shows on mobile when scrolling down a product page
- [ ] Cart-drawer upsell adds the cleaning kit
- [ ] WhatsApp button opens chat with the right number
- [ ] Product card badges show for `new`, `bestseller` and `polarized`
- [ ] "Buy it now" button turned OFF on the product page
- [ ] Filters and sorting work on collection pages
- [ ] 404 page, search results page and empty cart look fine

## Mobile & speed (most of your customers are on phones)
- [ ] Test on a real Android phone over mobile data, not just Wi-Fi
- [ ] Test on Chrome, Facebook in-app browser and Instagram in-app browser (where most ad clicks land)
- [ ] Text is readable and buttons are easy to tap
- [ ] PageSpeed Insights mobile score 50+ (Online Store → Themes shows Shopify's speed report)
- [ ] All images compressed (under 300 KB each)
- [ ] 10 apps or fewer installed; remove any unused ones

## Marketing & tracking
- [ ] Meta Pixel + Conversions API connected (Facebook & Instagram app), with a test purchase showing in Events Manager
- [ ] TikTok Pixel connected
- [ ] Google Analytics 4 + Search Console set up, sitemap submitted
- [ ] Welcome pop-up working, and signups appear in Customers
- [ ] Abandoned-checkout emails ON
- [ ] Facebook Page, Instagram and TikTok accounts created with the same handle, bio link to the store
- [ ] Instagram Shopping catalog approved

## Customer service
- [ ] WhatsApp Business app set up with greeting message, away message and quick replies (price, delivery, COD, return)
- [ ] COD order-confirmation script ready (call or WhatsApp within 2 hours)
- [ ] Process for checking prescriptions with your lens lab
- [ ] FAQ answers match your real policies

## Final test
- [ ] Place 3 complete test orders (one COD, one online payment, one prescription), then fulfill → cancel → refund each one to check the full flow
- [ ] Ask 3 friends to buy something on their phones and report any confusion
