## Technical SEO checklist
- Connect a custom domain (e.g. `shadeo.com.bd` or `shadeo.store`) and set it as primary
- Submit `yourdomain/sitemap.xml` in **Google Search Console**
- Create a **Google Business Profile** if you have a pickup point or showroom
- Compress images to under 300 KB (use 2000px wide, WebP/JPG)
- Every product: unique description, never copied from suppliers
- Link the blog articles to the matching collections
