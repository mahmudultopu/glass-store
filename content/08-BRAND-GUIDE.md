# SHADEO: Brand guide

## Brand in one line
**SHADEO makes premium eyewear for Bangladesh's bright, busy life, at honest prices.**

## Personality
Bold · Confident · Playful, not silly · Honest · Local pride, global style

## Voice
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Short, punchy lines: "Big frames. Bigger energy." | Long technical paragraphs |
| Talk like a stylish friend | Sound like a pharmacy or a discount shop |
| Use local references: Dhaka traffic, Cox's Bazar, Eid photos | Copy Western slang that doesn't land |
| Back up claims: "100% UV400, tested" | Claim "original", "best in BD" or numbers you can't prove |

---

## Logo concepts
Give these briefs to a designer, then test the top 2 with real customers.

1. **The "O" lens:** wordmark `SHADEO` in heavy geometric caps (Archivo Black style). The final **O** is a solid amber circle, like a sunlit lens. Works as a favicon on its own.
2. **Frame ligature:** the **D** and **E** joined by a small bridge line, so "DE" reads as a pair of glasses inside the word.
3. **Monogram S:** a bold "S" inside a rounded-square lens shape for the app icon, packaging stickers and Instagram profile picture.
4. **Sun-cut:** wordmark with a thin horizontal line cut through the top third of every letter, like a gradient lens tint.

**Rules:** black on off-white, off-white on black, black on amber. Never stretch it, never add drop shadows, and keep clear space equal to the height of the "S".

## Photography style
- **Product shots:** pure white or very light grey (`#F2F2F2`) background, soft shadow, front + 45° + side + folded views, same camera height for every product.
- **Lifestyle:** real Bangladeshi models, golden-hour sunlight, urban Dhaka (rooftops, rickshaws, cafés, Hatirjheel) and the coast (Cox's Bazar, Kuakata). Warm tones, natural skin, no heavy filters.
- **Hover image:** always a model wearing the frame, cropped from the nose up.
