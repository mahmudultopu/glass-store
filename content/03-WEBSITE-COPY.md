## FAQ PAGE (15 questions)

**Orders & delivery**
1. **How long does delivery take?**
   Inside Dhaka: 1–2 working days. Outside Dhaka: 2–4 working days. Prescription orders: 5–7 days.
2. **How much is delivery?**
   Tk 70 inside Dhaka, Tk 130 outside Dhaka. **Free on orders over Tk 3,000.**
3. **Do you offer Cash on Delivery?**
   Yes, all over Bangladesh. You can also pay by bKash, Nagad or card.
4. **Can I check the product before paying?**
   Yes. With COD you can open the parcel in front of the delivery person and check your frames before you pay.
5. **How do I track my order?**
   We send an SMS with your tracking link once it ships. You can also WhatsApp us your order number.

**Products & quality**
6. **Are your sunglasses really UV400?**
   Yes. Every SHADEO lens blocks 100% of UVA and UVB rays, and we test every batch with a UV meter.
7. **What does polarized mean?**
   Polarized lenses cut glare reflected off roads, water and glass. Everything looks sharper and your eyes relax. Great for driving and the beach.
8. **Do blue-light glasses really help?**
   They filter part of the blue light and glare from screens. Many people find their eyes feel less tired after long screen hours. They're not a medical treatment, though; see an eye doctor if you have ongoing eye problems.
9. **How do I know which size fits me?**
   Open the "Size guide" on any product page, or compare with the numbers printed inside a pair you already own. Or send us a selfie on WhatsApp and we'll help.

**Prescription**
10. **Can I get my prescription in any frame?**
    Yes, any frame marked "Prescription-ready". Pick your lens type on the product page.
11. **How do I send my prescription?**
    Type it in on the product page, or send a clear photo on WhatsApp after ordering. Our optician checks it before making your lenses.
12. **Do you make progressive or bifocal lenses?**
    Not yet. They're coming soon. We currently make single-vision lenses.

**Returns & warranty**
13. **What's your return policy?**
    7-day easy returns or exchanges for unused frames in original packaging. Prescription lenses are made to order and can't be returned for change of mind.
14. **What does the warranty cover?**
    6 months against manufacturing defects: broken hinges, peeling coating, loose frames (not accidental damage or scratches).
15. **Do you sell gift cards?**
    Yes! Digital gift cards from Tk 1,000 to Tk 10,000, delivered instantly by email.
