## Shipping Policy

**Where we deliver:** everywhere in Bangladesh.

**Delivery times** (working days; Friday and public holidays excluded)
| Area | Time | Charge |
|---|---|---|
| Inside Dhaka | 1–2 days | Tk 70 |
| Outside Dhaka | 2–4 days | Tk 130 |
| Prescription orders | 5–7 days | Same as above |
| Orders over Tk 3,000 | — | **FREE** |

**Processing:** orders placed before 3 PM ship the same day. Orders after 3 PM ship the next working day. We call or WhatsApp to confirm every Cash on Delivery order before shipping.

**Couriers:** we ship with [Steadfast / Pathao / RedX]. You'll get an SMS with a tracking link once your parcel leaves our warehouse.

**Checking your parcel:** with COD, you may open the parcel in front of the delivery person and check the product before paying. If anything is wrong, refuse the parcel and contact us; we'll sort it out.

**Failed delivery:** if you're unreachable after 2 attempts, the parcel returns to us. For repeated refused COD orders without a valid reason, we may ask for advance payment of the delivery charge on future orders.

## Return & Refund Policy

**7-day easy returns.** Not happy? Return or exchange within **7 days of delivery**.

**Eligible for return**
- Frames that are unused, unscratched, with all tags, the case and original packaging
- Wrong item sent, or arrived damaged or defective (report within 48 hours with photos)

**Not eligible**
- Frames fitted with **prescription lenses** (made to order). These are remade free if there's a lens defect or our mistake.
- Products showing wear, scratches or damage after delivery
- Items bought on final clearance (marked "Final Sale")

**How to return**
1. WhatsApp or email us your order number and reason within 7 days.
2. We'll arrange a pickup or give you the return address.
3. Once we receive and inspect the item (1–2 days), we process your exchange or refund.

**Refunds**
- **Our mistake / defective item:** full refund including delivery charges, or a free replacement.
- **Change of mind:** refund of the product price minus the delivery charge, or a free exchange (you pay the return delivery).
- Refunds go to your bKash / Nagad / bank account or original card within **5–7 working days**. COD orders are refunded by bKash, Nagad or bank transfer.

## Warranty Policy

Every SHADEO frame comes with a **6-month warranty** against manufacturing defects from the delivery date.

**Covered:** broken or loose hinges, frame cracks without impact, peeling lens coating, nose pads coming off, screws that won't stay tight.

**Not covered:** accidental damage (sitting on them, drops, pets), lens scratches from normal use, heat damage (e.g. left in a car), loss or theft, repairs done by others.

**How to claim:** WhatsApp us a photo or video of the issue and your order number. If covered, we repair or replace the frame free, including delivery.
