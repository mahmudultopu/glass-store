# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are listed here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [2.0.0] - 2026-10-28

### Changed
- Complete redesign in a "clean luxury" style: ivory and sand palette, Cormorant Garamond and Jost typography, photo-led layouts.
- Product drawings replaced with real product photos, plus lifestyle photos of women, men and children.
- New header with centred logo and Women / Men / Kids / Optical / Sale navigation and photo mega menus.
- Homepage rebuilt: full-width hero, category tiles, Kids and Optical feature sections, lookbook, "help me choose" cards.
- Product gallery now shows the product photo, a close-up and a styled look.

### Added
- Kids collection: four kids sunglasses (including a 3-pair pack) and bendable kids eyeglasses.
- Women and Men collections with full-width photo banners.
- Browline frame shape, a leather pouch accessory and more frames (27 products in total).
- Fade-in animations as sections scroll into view, with reduced-motion support.
- Professional README with screenshots, this changelog, `.gitattributes` and `.editorconfig`.

### Fixed
- Mobile header no longer pushes the bag icon off-screen on narrow phones.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-28

### Added
- Static storefront: home, shop with filters and search, product pages with lens selector and prescription form, cart drawer, checkout with WhatsApp confirmation, quiz, order tracking and info pages.
- Shopify Dawn theme kit: custom sections and snippets with a step-by-step setup guide.
- Business docs: website copy, policies, SEO, brand guide, launch checklist and 30-day marketing plan.
- Vercel deployment from the `website/` folder.
