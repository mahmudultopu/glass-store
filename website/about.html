About SHADEO

We make seeing look good.

SHADEO started with a simple frustration. In Bangladesh, you either paid a fortune for imported brand-name sunglasses, or bought cheap pairs that broke in a month and didn't really protect your eyes.

We knew there had to be a better way. So we started designing our own frames, with premium materials, real UV400 protection and honest prices. We skip the middlemen, the big retail markups and the fake "original" tags, and sell directly to you.
