Shipping policy

Processing: orders placed before 3 PM ship the same working day; later orders ship the next working day. Friday and public holidays are not working days. We call or WhatsApp to confirm every Cash on Delivery order before shipping.

Tracking: you'll get an SMS with a tracking link once your parcel leaves our warehouse.

Checking your parcel: with Cash on Delivery, you may open the parcel in front of the delivery person and check the product before paying. If anything is wrong, refuse the parcel and contact us; we'll sort it out.

Failed delivery: if you're unreachable after 2 attempts, the parcel returns to us. After repeated refused COD orders without a valid reason, we may ask for the delivery charge in advance on future orders.

Return & refund policy

7-day easy returns. Not happy? Return or exchange within 7 days of delivery.

Eligible

  • Unused, unscratched frames with tags, case and original packaging
  • Wrong item, or arrived damaged or defective (report within 48 hours with photos)

Not eligible

  • Frames fitted with prescription lenses (made to order). We remake them free if there's a lens defect or our mistake.
  • Products with wear, scratches or damage after delivery
  • Items marked "Final Sale"

How to return

  1. WhatsApp or email us your order number and reason within 7 days.
  2. We'll arrange a pickup or share the return address.
  3. Once we receive and inspect the item (1–2 days), we process your exchange or refund.

Refunds

  • Our mistake / defective item: full refund including delivery, or a free replacement.
  • Change of mind: product price refunded minus delivery charges, or a free exchange (you pay return delivery).
  • Refunds are sent to your bKash, Nagad or bank account within 5–7 working days.

Warranty

Every SHADEO frame has a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects from the delivery date.

Covered: broken or loose hinges, frame cracks without impact, peeling lens coating, nose pads coming off, screws that won't stay tight.

Not covered: accidental damage (sitting on them, drops, pets), lens scratches from normal use, heat damage, loss or theft, or repairs done by others.

How to claim: WhatsApp us a photo or video of the issue with your order number. If it's covered, we repair or replace the frame free, including delivery.

Privacy policy

What we collect: your name, phone number, email, delivery address, order history, prescription details (if you share them), and website usage data through cookies and similar technologies.

Why: to process and deliver orders, make prescription lenses, provide support, send order updates, and, if you agree, send marketing messages.

Prescription data is used only to make your lenses, is shared only with our lens lab, and is never used for marketing.

Sharing: we share delivery details with our courier partner and payment details with payment providers. We never sell your personal data.

Your choices: unsubscribe from marketing at any time, and ask us to see, correct or delete your data by emailing .
