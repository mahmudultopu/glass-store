How to read your prescription

TermMeaning
OD / OSRight eye / left eye
SPHSphere: lens power. Minus (−) = short-sighted, plus (+) = long-sighted
CYLCylinder: corrects astigmatism (may be blank)
AXISDirection of the cylinder, 0–180 (only if CYL is given)
PDPupillary distance: the space between your pupils in mm (usually 55–70)

Good to know

  • We currently make single-vision lenses (for near- or far-sightedness). Progressive and bifocal lenses are coming soon.
  • Your prescription should be less than 2 years old.
  • Need an eye test first? Visit any registered optometrist or eye hospital, then send us the prescription.
  • Prescription lenses are made to order, so they can't be returned for change of mind. If there's a lens defect or our mistake, we remake them free.
