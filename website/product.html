      Which size fits me?

      1. Check glasses you already own. Look inside the arm for numbers like 52□18-140: lens width, bridge and temple length in mm. Pick a SHADEO frame within ±2 mm.
      2. The card trick. Hold any bank card flat under your nose, one short edge at the centre of your nose. If it reaches past the outer corner of your eye, choose Small. If it ends right at the corner, Medium. If it stops before the corner, Large.
      3. Still not sure? Send us a selfie on WhatsApp and we'll recommend a size.
