Orders & delivery

How long does delivery take?
Inside Dhaka: 1–2 working days. Outside Dhaka: 2–4 working days. Prescription orders: 5–7 days. Friday and public holidays are not counted.
How much is delivery?
Tk 70 inside Dhaka and Tk 130 outside Dhaka. Free on orders over Tk 3,000.
Do you offer Cash on Delivery?
Yes, all over Bangladesh. You can also pay by bKash or Nagad.
Can I check the product before paying?
Yes. With Cash on Delivery you can open the parcel in front of the delivery person and check your frames before you pay.
How do I track my order?
We send an SMS with your tracking link once your order ships. You can also use the Track Order page or WhatsApp us your order number.

Products & quality

Are your sunglasses really UV400?
Yes. Every SHADEO lens is made to block 100% of UVA and UVB rays (UV400). That's real protection, not just a dark tint.
What does polarized mean?
Polarized lenses cut glare reflected off roads, water and glass. Everything looks sharper and your eyes relax. Great for driving and the beach.
Do blue-light glasses really help?
They filter part of the blue light and glare from screens, and many people find their eyes feel less tired after long screen hours. They're not a medical treatment, though; please see an eye doctor if you have ongoing eye problems.
How do I know which size fits me?
Every product page shows the exact measurements and a size guide. You can also compare with the numbers printed inside a pair you already own, or send us a selfie on WhatsApp.

Prescription

Can I get my prescription in any frame?
Yes, any frame marked "Prescription-ready". Choose your lens type on the product page. See how it works.
How do I send my prescription?
Type it in on the product page, or send a clear photo on WhatsApp after ordering. Our optician checks it before making your lenses.
Do you make progressive or bifocal lenses?
Not yet; they're coming soon. We currently make single-vision lenses.

Returns & warranty

What's your return policy?
7-day easy returns or exchanges for unused frames in original packaging. Prescription lenses are made to order and can't be returned for change of mind. Full policy.
What does the warranty cover?
6 months against manufacturing defects: broken hinges, peeling coating, loose frames. It doesn't cover accidental damage or scratches. Warranty details.
